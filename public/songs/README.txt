SONG BADALNA HO TO
  Apni MP3 file isi naam se yahan rakh dein (masalan monday.mp3) aur
  src/music.js mein us din ka title badal dein.
  Agar saaton songs apne laga lein to src/music.js mein MUSIC_CREDIT = null kar dein.
  Sirf wohi songs lagayein jin ko use karne ki aap ko ijazat ho.
